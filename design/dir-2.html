Big Shoulders poster type, Noto Kufi Arabic, IBM Plex body. Magenta acts, green means quiet, amber means pressure.

Why Sharjah

Every color is already on the lagoon: the Al Majaz fountain's colored jets, sodium lamps along the corniche, green reflections off Khalid Lagoon and the Al Qasba canal, Al Noor Island's lit installations, and the Sharjah Light Festival projections on facades. Deep navy is the water. Big Shoulders gives headlines the register of a concert bill for Amphitheatre nights.

Why sustainable

Off-peak, after-dark visiting is the sustainability lever: cooler hours, walking the corniche instead of driving, and spreading visitors beyond crowded daytime sites. The palette is built from light already switched on, so the brand adds no spectacle. Green marks quiet hours and walk routes; amber marks crowd pressure.

Risks

A dark, saturated interface is hard to read on a phone outdoors at 14:00 on the AUS lawn and in a bright judging room; keep ink at 16:1, keep the amber and green at 10:1 or better, and test the demo under sunlight before the pitch. Magenta on navy slides toward nightlife or casino if the type turns playful; keep Big Shoulders uppercase and the copy civic. Camera access and WebXR in iOS Safari are fragile, so the Look lens should degrade to a still-photo overlay for the live demo. Noto Kufi Arabic at the same size as Big Shoulders will not share a baseline without the 0.92 scale and a separate line-height, so mixed-script headlines need a test render before the slides are built. The three references were confirmed only by gallery tags and page copy, not by screenshots, because the browser pane refused external navigation in this session.
