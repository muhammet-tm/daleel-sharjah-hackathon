At Al Hisn Fort, 09:42, 34°C

Why was the fort demolished in 1969?

Daleel

In 1969 most of Al Hisn was pulled down to make room for the modern city center. One tower was saved, and the fort was later rebuilt from old photographs and plans. After a full restoration it reopened as a museum in 2015.
