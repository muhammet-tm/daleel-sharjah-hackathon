Cormorant and Reem Kufi for display, IBM Plex body, Plex Mono for data. Brass is stroke only; verdigris marks every sustainability figure.

Why Sharjah

Heart of Sharjah houses use coral stone, gypsum, teak doors, barjeel wind towers, and lattice screens; the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization holds the geometry; Souk Al Arsah sells the brass and copperware; Central Souk is tiled in blue; Khalid Lagoon supplies the teal. The palette and the lattice come from these specific places.

Why sustainable

The mashrabiya is passive cooling: shade and airflow with no electricity. The interface uses it the same way, letting through only the information a walker needs, and extends it to shade routes, off-peak slots, and crowd dispersal. Dark teal saves OLED power; line-drawn AR replaces heavy 3D downloads.

Risks

Reads as a mosque brochure or luxury hotel if Cormorant gets too large or italic; hold display to two sizes and never italic. SVG mask animation can stutter on low-end Android in a browser demo; precompute one mask per sheet position and animate only the mask scale. The lattice steals camera area in the half-open state, so test that the top 55 percent stays usable for orientation. Brass on surface teal is 4.10:1, so brass text on surfaces must be 14px bold or larger; coral is for shapes and large labels only. RTL mirroring needs a symmetric 8-fold module, so do not switch to a 10-fold girih late. The pattern is shared heritage, but avoid cropping mosque imagery with the lattice in ways that read as disrespect.
