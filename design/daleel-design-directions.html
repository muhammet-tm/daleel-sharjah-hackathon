Caveats before choosing

  • 01 Route Band Signage. Highest scoring and the easiest to build in hours (flat plates, no textures). The risk is sternness: the photography and the Arabic plates have to carry the warmth, or it reads as a transport utility.
  • 02 Lagoon After Dark. The strongest story for the sustainability lever (after-dark, off-peak, walking). Dark saturated screens are hard to read on a phone outdoors and in a bright judging room; test under sunlight before committing.
  • 03 Hashiya Folio. Ties directly to World Book Capital and the calligraphy heritage, and no other team will show this. Margin notes need one sentence of explanation in the pitch, and the AR plate must be the first screen shown or it reads as a brochure.
  • 04 Girih Screen. The most distinctive AR treatment (line-drawn overlays through a lattice mask). The mask animation can stutter on low-end Android in a browser; precompute masks. Hold the serif to two sizes or it drifts toward a hotel brochure.
  • 05 Mleiha Field Sheet. Caution: its passport stamps and ticket stubs echo Sharjah Quest, the 2025 winner built by two of this year's mentors (digital stamps, badges). If chosen, replace stamps with trench-style find codes and keep the ledger; the map-first survey sheet itself is sound.
00 · Daleel brand

Daleel brand

Look Up.

The team's identity: octagonal compass mark with a brass needle and a location pin, navy wordmark in Latin and Arabic, cream ground.

  • #F7F3EAbackground
  • #E6DCC8surface
  • #C9A24Daccent
  • #14213Dink
  • #8A6A1Fderived
Daleelدليل

Navy carries every word; gold marks the one choice Daleel recommends (the needle, the primary button, the active tab). Type is a provisional match for the wordmark: Marcellus and Noto Naskh Arabic for display, IBM Plex Sans and Plex Sans Arabic for body.

Why Sharjah

The octagon follows the eight-fold geometry of Sharjah's heritage screens and tiles, and the compass needle is the guide in the name. Navy and brass sit with the coral stone and gypsum of the Heart of Sharjah without a trend palette.

Why sustainable

The needle is the product: it points visitors to a better time and a quieter place. Gold marks the recommended choice and navy carries the facts, so the nudge reads at a glance.

Risks

Gold on cream is 2.17:1 and gold on sand 1.76:1, so gold can never carry text on light grounds: use navy, or dark brass #8A6A1F for gold-toned text. The type here is provisional (Marcellus and Noto Naskh Arabic as stand-ins for the wordmark); swap in the fonts Rama used. The mark is drawn as a plain octagon placeholder until the logo file is in design/brand/.

01 · Route Band Signage

Route Band Signage

Sharjah, signposted by footprint.

Civic wayfinding signage (airport and museum information design, Schiphol and AIGA/DOT pictogram lineage)

  • #F2F2F0background
  • #14211Bsurface
  • #101513ink
  • #0F6B3Faccent
  • #D8761Csecondary accent
  • #1F4E8CBlue Souk tile blue
Al Hisn Fort
حصن الشارقة

Overpass plates, Atkinson Hyperlegible body, Readex Pro Arabic. Bands encode footprint: green low, blue water, amber heat and crowd.

Why Sharjah

The three route bands take their colors from places on the routes themselves: Blue Souk tile blue, Kalba mangrove green, and Mleiha's amber rock. The neutral board recalls the whitewashed courtyards of the Sharjah Art Foundation and the coral-stone of the Heart of Sharjah. Bilingual plates follow the road and museum signs visitors already read along the Corniche.

Why sustainable

Every route band is a footprint label: green for walking and shaded paths, blue for creek water transport, amber for motorized or peak-heat legs. Crowd gauges borrow the parking-sign idiom to disperse visitors toward Mleiha, Kalba, and Khor Fakkan. Flat print-grade graphics run on low-end phones without heavy rendering.

Risks

Judges may read the sternness as a transport utility rather than a tourism product, so the photography has to carry the warmth in the deck. Eight bespoke pictograms is the ceiling in seven hours; the fallback is Material Symbols with credit in the disclosure document. Overpass has tight apertures below 14px, so small labels must use Atkinson. Readex Pro is a legibility face, not a calligraphic one, and Arabic-first judges may find it plain. Two contrast rules must live in the CSS tokens: amber never carries white text (3.22:1) and green text never sits on the dark plate (2.53:1). Three band colors can collide with basemap colors, so the map base must stay desaturated gray.

02 · Lagoon After Dark

Lagoon After Dark

Sharjah after dark, on foot.

Civic night poster: event-bill typography, flat saturated accents on deep navy, documentary night photography

  • #0B1530background
  • #16234Dsurface
  • #F1F3F8ink
  • #FF5C99accent
  • #2EE6A6secondary accent
  • #FFB454sodium lamp amber
After sunset, on foot
الشارقة بعد الغروب

Big Shoulders poster type, Noto Kufi Arabic, IBM Plex body. Magenta acts, green means quiet, amber means pressure.

Why Sharjah

Every color is already on the lagoon: the Al Majaz fountain's colored jets, sodium lamps along the corniche, green reflections off Khalid Lagoon and the Al Qasba canal, Al Noor Island's lit installations, and the Sharjah Light Festival projections on facades. Deep navy is the water. Big Shoulders gives headlines the register of a concert bill for Amphitheatre nights.

Why sustainable

Off-peak, after-dark visiting is the sustainability lever: cooler hours, walking the corniche instead of driving, and spreading visitors beyond crowded daytime sites. The palette is built from light already switched on, so the brand adds no spectacle. Green marks quiet hours and walk routes; amber marks crowd pressure.

Risks

A dark, saturated interface is hard to read on a phone outdoors at 14:00 on the AUS lawn and in a bright judging room; keep ink at 16:1, keep the amber and green at 10:1 or better, and test the demo under sunlight before the pitch. Magenta on navy slides toward nightlife or casino if the type turns playful; keep Big Shoulders uppercase and the copy civic. Camera access and WebXR in iOS Safari are fragile, so the Look lens should degrade to a still-photo overlay for the live demo. Noto Kufi Arabic at the same size as Big Shoulders will not share a baseline without the 0.92 scale and a separate line-height, so mixed-script headlines need a test render before the slides are built. The three references were confirmed only by gallery tags and page copy, not by screenshots, because the browser pane refused external navigation in this session.

03 · Hashiya Folio

Hashiya Folio

Sharjah, read in the margins.

Scholarly editorial, manuscript-derived typographic UI: paper and ink with rubricated hierarchy and marginal commentary

  • #EFEFEDbackground
  • #FBFBFAsurface
  • #1B1917ink
  • #9E2B18accent
  • #22407Asecondary accent
  • #5C5853rule gray
Sharjah, read in the margins
الشارقة في الحواشي

EB Garamond and Amiri for display, IBM Plex for body, Plex Mono for plate captions only. Rubric red marks notes and actions.

Why Sharjah

Sharjah was UNESCO World Book Capital 2019 and hosts the Sharjah International Book Fair and House of Wisdom library. The heritage area holds the Sharjah Calligraphy Museum and Al Hisn Fort's coral-stone walls. Amiri revives the Bulaq Press naskh of early Arabic printing. Rubric red and lapis come from manuscript illumination, not from a trend palette.

Why sustainable

The margin keeps a ledger: meters walked, bus rides, dirhams spent with souq craftsmen, water refills. The day closes with a colophon page that totals it. AR annotates existing walls instead of rendering replacements, and the page is text and hairlines, so it loads on weak signal with low energy.

Risks

Judges may read paper and ink as a brochure rather than a product, so the AR plate must be the first thing shown in the pitch. Marginalia is unfamiliar; the five-minute pitch needs one sentence explaining the matn and hashiya idea. Amiri and EB Garamond have different vertical metrics, so Arabic and English need separate line-height tokens or lines collide. Rule gray passes at 6.1:1, but hairlines thinner than 1px vanish in direct sun. The team must resist cream palette drift and the temptation to add a 3D reconstruction, which breaks both the look and the seven-hour budget.

04 · Girih Screen

Girih Screen

Sharjah through the lattice: light in, heat out.

Islamic geometric lattice as structure: dark heritage editorial with drafting-line AR, not ornament

  • #0F3D3Ebackground
  • #175152surface
  • #F1E9D6ink
  • #D1AA4Faccent
  • #D27A5Asecondary accent
  • #86C3B2data
Light in, heat out
من خلف المشربية

Cormorant and Reem Kufi for display, IBM Plex body, Plex Mono for data. Brass is stroke only; verdigris marks every sustainability figure.

Why Sharjah

Heart of Sharjah houses use coral stone, gypsum, teak doors, barjeel wind towers, and lattice screens; the Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization holds the geometry; Souk Al Arsah sells the brass and copperware; Central Souk is tiled in blue; Khalid Lagoon supplies the teal. The palette and the lattice come from these specific places.

Why sustainable

The mashrabiya is passive cooling: shade and airflow with no electricity. The interface uses it the same way, letting through only the information a walker needs, and extends it to shade routes, off-peak slots, and crowd dispersal. Dark teal saves OLED power; line-drawn AR replaces heavy 3D downloads.

Risks

Reads as a mosque brochure or luxury hotel if Cormorant gets too large or italic; hold display to two sizes and never italic. SVG mask animation can stutter on low-end Android in a browser demo; precompute one mask per sheet position and animate only the mask scale. The lattice steals camera area in the half-open state, so test that the top 55 percent stays usable for orientation. Brass on surface teal is 4.10:1, so brass text on surfaces must be 14px bold or larger; coral is for shapes and large labels only. RTL mirroring needs a symmetric 8-fold module, so do not switch to a 10-fold girih late. The pattern is shared heritage, but avoid cropping mosque imagery with the lattice in ways that read as disrespect.

05 · Mleiha Field Sheet

Mleiha Field Sheet

Walk Sharjah like a field survey: map first, notes in the margin, a stamp for every lighter trip.

Field-notebook editorial, map-first survey sheet

  • #E4E7E3background
  • #FFFFFFsurface
  • #1C1A17ink
  • #9E3E18accent
  • #1F5F4Esecondary accent
  • #8F1D21stamp red
Map first, notes in the margin
دفتر الميدان

Fraunces with Amiri, IBM Plex body, Plex Mono capitals for codes and stamps. Oxide for waypoints, mangrove green for protected areas.

Why Sharjah

Mleiha's archaeology center labels finds by trench and context code, which becomes the app's label grammar. The sheet color comes from Kalba mangrove and Wasit Wetland survey maps, the accent from the iron oxide of Fossil Rock at evening, and the stamp red from passport entry stamps. Coral stone walls of the Heritage Area set the white card texture.

Why sustainable

The notebook works as a ledger. Each ticket stub records the travel mode and the carbon saved against a private car; stamps are issued only at protected or low-crowd sites such as Kalba mangroves and Wasit Wetland; the map hatches off-peak hours so visitors spread out across the emirate.

Risks

Skeuomorphism reads as nostalgic if texture is overdone; keep the paper white and let structure (rules, perforation, stamps) carry the feel. Recoloring a MapLibre style eats time, so start from OpenFreeMap Positron and change six layer colors only. Arabic right-to-left flips the margin notes and index tabs; test with Plex Sans Arabic on day one. Judges may read the notebook as the gimmick and miss the ledger, so lead the pitch with the carbon and crowd ledger and show the stamp second. Every Commons photo must be credited in the disclosure.

After you choose

  1. Rama drops the logo files (SVG preferred) into design/brand/ and names the wordmark fonts, so the provisional Marcellus and Noto Naskh stand-ins can be swapped.
  2. One Higgsfield hero image from the direction's prompt (and up to three variants), then one five-second clip for the pitch opening; every asset is logged in the AI Usage Disclosure.
  3. The direction's tokens are applied to the three demo screens (Explore, AR view, Guide) in the deployed app, then the transition recipes for the itinerary swap and the guide reply.
  4. The pitch deck inherits the same tokens so the phone and the slides look like one product.
