EB Garamond and Amiri for display, IBM Plex for body, Plex Mono for plate captions only. Rubric red marks notes and actions.

Why Sharjah

Sharjah was UNESCO World Book Capital 2019 and hosts the Sharjah International Book Fair and House of Wisdom library. The heritage area holds the Sharjah Calligraphy Museum and Al Hisn Fort's coral-stone walls. Amiri revives the Bulaq Press naskh of early Arabic printing. Rubric red and lapis come from manuscript illumination, not from a trend palette.

Why sustainable

The margin keeps a ledger: meters walked, bus rides, dirhams spent with souq craftsmen, water refills. The day closes with a colophon page that totals it. AR annotates existing walls instead of rendering replacements, and the page is text and hairlines, so it loads on weak signal with low energy.

Risks

Judges may read paper and ink as a brochure rather than a product, so the AR plate must be the first thing shown in the pitch. Marginalia is unfamiliar; the five-minute pitch needs one sentence explaining the matn and hashiya idea. Amiri and EB Garamond have different vertical metrics, so Arabic and English need separate line-height tokens or lines collide. Rule gray passes at 6.1:1, but hairlines thinner than 1px vanish in direct sun. The team must resist cream palette drift and the temptation to add a 3D reconstruction, which breaks both the look and the seven-hour budget.
