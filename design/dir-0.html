Daleelدليل

Navy carries every word; gold marks the one choice Daleel recommends (the needle, the primary button, the active tab). Type is a provisional match for the wordmark: Marcellus and Noto Naskh Arabic for display, IBM Plex Sans and Plex Sans Arabic for body.

Why Sharjah

The octagon follows the eight-fold geometry of Sharjah's heritage screens and tiles, and the compass needle is the guide in the name. Navy and brass sit with the coral stone and gypsum of the Heart of Sharjah without a trend palette.

Why sustainable

The needle is the product: it points visitors to a better time and a quieter place. Gold marks the recommended choice and navy carries the facts, so the nudge reads at a glance.

Risks

Gold on cream is 2.17:1 and gold on sand 1.76:1, so gold can never carry text on light grounds: use navy, or dark brass #8A6A1F for gold-toned text. The type here is provisional (Marcellus and Noto Naskh Arabic as stand-ins for the wordmark); swap in the fonts Rama used. The mark is drawn as a plain octagon placeholder until the logo file is in design/brand/.
